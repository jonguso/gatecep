# PC-030M13E — Distinct Market Rankings

- Equities: complete verified NSE universe, alphabetically sorted.
- Gainers: top 10 positive percentage movers, highest first.
- Losers: top 5 negative percentage movers, steepest decline first.
- Volume: top 10 securities with positive verified traded volume.
- Turnover: top 5 securities with positive verified traded value.
- Zero, null, and unavailable activity values are excluded from Volume and Turnover.
- An explicit evidence-unavailable message replaces repeated Equities results when activity data is missing.
