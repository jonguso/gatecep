Privacy Policy

Updated September 12, 2026

This policy describes GateCEP's intended handling of information used to provide account, portfolio, broker-linking, analytics, goal-planning and AI-assisted features.

Information

Depending on enabled features, GateCEP may process account/profile information, investor preferences, portfolio holdings, cash and transaction information, broker-link/synchronization evidence, goals, app activity and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Use

Information is used to provide requested product features, maintain account and portfolio records, generate analytics and Coach G assistance, support synchronization/reconciliation, improve reliability, and meet security or legal obligations.

Broker and provider data

When broker or market-data integrations are enabled, information may be exchanged with the relevant provider as necessary for the requested integration and subject to that provider's terms.

Security and retention

GateCEP is designed to protect user information with appropriate technical and organizational controls. Retention should be limited to operational, legal, security and recordkeeping needs.

UAT

GateCEP is currently in UAT/release stabilization. Test environments should not be assumed to provide the same availability or production guarantees as a released production service.

This page is a product policy summary and should be reviewed by qualified legal/privacy counsel before production launch.
