Risk Disclosure

Updated September 12, 2026

Investing and trading securities involves risk, including loss of principal. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Coach G and analytics

Coach G recommendations, scores, scenarios, forecasts, benchmark comparisons and other AI-assisted outputs are informational decision-support tools and are not individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Market and execution risk

Prices can change rapidly. Quotes may be delayed. Liquidity, slippage, fees, broker availability, order rejection, partial fills, outages and reconciliation differences can affect actual results.

Historical information

GateCEP is designed not to fabricate REAL portfolio history. When evidence is insufficient, analytics may be unavailable or shown as N/A. Historical results remain dependent on the accuracy and completeness of underlying records.

Practice versus REAL

Practice portfolios and simulations are hypothetical. They do not represent actual holdings or executed broker trades. Only confirmed broker execution evidence should be treated as an executed transaction.

User responsibility

Consider your objectives, financial circumstances, time horizon and risk tolerance, and seek appropriately licensed professional advice when needed.
