# PC-030M20AR — Unified Coach G Decision Conversation

Adds a common, advisory-only scenario contract and a conversational entry experience to Trading. It supports direct decisions, Coach G recovery recommendations, open what-if/new-money questions, and outside security ideas. Investor reason and decision priority travel with the scenario into the existing detailed Trade Lab. Missing REAL evidence is displayed as unavailable rather than fabricated zero data.

This increment establishes the conversation and multi-entry handoff. The existing detailed `/trade` engine remains responsible for transaction-specific BUY/SELL calculations. No REAL or Practice portfolio mutation is introduced.
