# PC-030M16C — Exclusive Market panels

Markets now displays one primary content panel at a time:

- Market Results (Equities, Summary, Gainers, Losers, Volume, or Turnover)
- Indices
- Watchlist

Opening Indices or Watchlist hides the current market-results panel. Selecting any market tab restores Market Results. Closing an expanded panel also restores Market Results.

Run `bash scripts/verify-pc030m16c-exclusive-market-panels.sh` from `mobile`.
